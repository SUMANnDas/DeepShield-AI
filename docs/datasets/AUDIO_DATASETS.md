# Audio Dataset Review

| Dataset | Distribution and attacks | Audio details | Access / rights / storage | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ASVspoof 2019 | LA has 121,461 trials: 12,483 bona fide and 108,978 spoofed. LA includes TTS and voice-conversion attacks. The full challenge also has replay/physical-access PA. | FLAC, sourced from PCM; 16 kHz, 16-bit in the official challenge paper. VCTK source speakers are split by speaker across train/dev/eval. Protocol files carry trial, speaker, and attack labels. | Edinburgh DataShare lists LA.zip (7.12 GB), PA.zip (16.45 GB), README and LICENSE_text. LICENSE_text applies ODC Attribution terms to the database, but expressly does not grant rights to individual contents; review speech-content rights and the actual release files before use. | Recommended primary training source using LA only, subject to content-rights review; preserve its official train/dev/eval protocol and unknown-attack distinction. |
| ASVspoof 2021 | Three evaluation tasks: LA (TTS/VC with channel effects), PA (replay in physical spaces), and DF (TTS/VC deepfake speech). Bona-fide and spoof labels/metadata are provided separately. | Official plan states FLAC, 16 kHz, 16-bit audio. Protocol and evaluation keys identify trial and attack metadata. | Official page states ODC Attribution. LA evaluation archive is 7.8 GB; DF evaluation archives total 34.5 GB. The challenge released evaluation data, not new training/development sets. | Suitable held-out evaluation, not training. Do not tune thresholds or select models on the final evaluation set. |
| Fake-or-Real (FoR) | More than 195,000 utterances across human and generated speech; paper reports roughly 111k real and 87k synthetic. Synthetic sources include Deep Voice 3 and WaveNet TTS; real sources include Arctic, LJ Speech, VoxForge and lab recordings. | Four releases: original, normalized, 2-second, and rerecorded. The normalized release balances class/gender and normalizes sample rate, volume, and channels. Original release is unmodified/mixed source material; inspect exact sample rates and formats. | York University distributes version archives. A clear dataset license and byte sizes are not stated on the dataset page; confirm terms with the provider before use. | Potential domain-diverse development set. Source-speaker metadata is not a unified guarantee; do not claim speaker-disjoint splits unless mapped metadata supports it. |
| WaveFake | Version 1.2.0 lists 104,885 generated clips and about 175 hours. Generators include MelGAN variants, Parallel WaveGAN, WaveGlow, HiFi-GAN and a TTS pipeline; English and Japanese sources. The reference real corpora are not redistributed in the generated archive. | 16-bit PCM WAV. The official page does not provide a single sample-rate statement for every file; inspect headers. Dataset is fake-only in the archive. | CC BY-SA 4.0. Current Zenodo v1.2.0 generated_audio.zip is 28.9 GB. A prior Zenodo record/version lists a different count and 23.4 GB; pin version 1.2.0 if approved. | Strong synthetic-domain evaluation/development material, but pair only with a separately licensed genuine set and keep generator labels. |

Unknown or version-dependent sample rates, duration distributions, speaker metadata and licenses must be read from the actual approved release, not assumed from a repack or another version.
