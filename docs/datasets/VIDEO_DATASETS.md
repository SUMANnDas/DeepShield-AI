# Video Dataset Review

| Dataset | Videos and labels | Manipulations / annotations | Format and resolution | Rights, access, and storage | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FaceForensics++ | 1,000 source sequences with manipulated versions; four manipulation families. | Deepfakes, Face2Face, FaceSwap, NeuralTextures; masks and compression variants are available. | H.264 video and extracted image material; c0/c23/c40. No single source resolution is guaranteed. | Approved request; non-commercial research/education terms. About 10 GB c23 videos, 500 GB c0, and about 2 TB for all PNG frames. | Recommended primary controlled face-video source. Use a subset and split by source video/identity. |
| Celeb-DF v2 | 590 real celebrity videos, 5,639 corresponding synthesized videos, plus 300 YouTube-real clips in the author's folder structure. | Improved face-swapping synthesis; author provides a test-video list. | Author distributes video clips; codec and a uniform resolution are not stated in the README. Its subjects and source collection differ from FF++. |
| DFDC | Meta lists a 5k-video preview and a 124k-video full set. Full set includes eight modification algorithms. | Face modification methods; source participants were paid actors who agreed to likeness manipulation. | Video files with metadata/protocols through the AWS/Kaggle route; exact per-file codec/resolution should be measured after access. | Requires AWS account, IAM user/access keys, and account number for access. Meta does not state a byte size on its page; an institutional competition presentation reports roughly 500 GB for about 130k clips. | Too large for current C: free space. Do not request/download full release for this prototype. |
| DeeperForensics-1.0 | 48,475 source videos and 11,000 manipulated videos. The dataset's target real videos are not included and must be sourced separately from FF++. | DF-VAE face swaps; seven real-world perturbation types at five levels, including mixed perturbations. About 164 GB sources + 119 GB manipulated + metadata, around 284 GB total; 300 GB or more recommended. | Robustness benchmark but not suitable on the current C: drive, and the missing real targets complicate use. |

## Sampling and leakage constraints

Frame labels inherit from the source video or its annotated temporal segment. Never randomly distribute extracted frames across splits. Group at least by original video; where identity is reliably available, group by identity. If identities are missing, report video-disjoint, not identity-disjoint, evaluation.

The preprocessing pipeline samples a bounded number of frames. It records source video ID, frame number, timestamp, label, face status, and simple IoU-linked face track IDs. Scene-change and face-presence selection are sampling heuristics, not annotation evidence.
